# Archetypes

Starting values for the brief. Every number is a default to tune by measurement and by ear, not a rule. Pick the closest row, copy it, and change what the brief demands.

| | Dreamy ambient (space, sleep) | Lo-fi chill (study, stream) | Driving (arcade, racing, action) | Focus pulse (work, flow) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tempo | 55–70 BPM, or free | 70–90 | 140–175 | 90–110 |
| Mode | Lydian, Dorian | Natural minor, Dorian, jazz minor 7ths and 9ths | Minor, Phrygian, harmonic minor | Mixolydian, Dorian |
| Chord length | 4–16 bars | 1–2 bars | 1–2 bars | 2–4 bars |
| Drums | none, or a very soft pulse | boom-bap, ghost notes, swing | four-on-the-floor or breakbeat | light, steady, no fills |
| Lead | sparse bells, soft sines | a pentatonic motif on a soft pluck | pulse or saw lead, syncopated | one repeating cell, little variation |
| Master low-pass | 2.5–4 kHz | 1.8–3 kHz | 4–7 kHz | 3–5 kHz |
| Reverb | 4–10 s hall, dark | 1–2 s, short | short room | short or none |
| Section length | minutes | 4–8 bar blocks, 30–36 bar pieces | 4–16 bar sections | long, almost no sections |
| Silence | frequent | between phrases | rare | rare |
| Variation comes from | independent layers, rare events, a slow arc | a soft independent layer, rests, a changed second half | phrase ends (a fill, a turnaround bar) and section changes; a locked riff | almost none |
| Loudness target | about -20 to -14 LUFS | about -12 LUFS | loudest | quiet |
| Danger | shrill bells, muddy reverb | harsh hats, a static loop | fatigue, ear-piercing leads, a riff that is randomised or a groove that drops out at the loop point | distraction, any surprise |

## Picking and bending

- When the brief says "dreamy" or "space", start from the ambient column and keep the register low: a calm track's sustained voices belong under about C6, and nothing in it should have energy above 2 kHz that the listener could single out.
- When the brief says "stream" or "all day", take the longest chord length and the least dense section the column allows, then add the long-form layers of step 5.
- When two columns fit, combine on one axis only: the mode of one with the tempo of another. A track that borrows from three archetypes has no character.
- When the track follows app state, decide which two columns are its calm and its busy ends and interpolate density and brightness between them; do not interpolate mode or tempo mid-bar.
